    Where a slug goes

    For a page called “Summer Menu”, use summer-menu as the final segment of a URL such as /journal/summer-menu. The tool keeps letters from different writing systems, uses hyphens between words and shows the encoded segment for links.

    When two titles produce the same segment, make one title more specific and generate again. Confirm the final path in your CMS: this check covers duplicates in this batch only. Google’s URL structure guidance
