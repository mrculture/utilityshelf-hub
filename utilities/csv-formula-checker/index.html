    Why a quoted field can still matter

    In the example, "=SUM(2,3)" is one CSV field. After the spreadsheet reads the quotes, the value starts with an equals sign. Confirm that the sender intended a formula; otherwise import that column as text. The signed amount -12.50 also merits a look even though it may be an ordinary refund.

    The checker reads the header and every field as text in this tab. It flags formula prefixes, including full-width variants and leading controls. Spreadsheet behaviour varies with application, locale and import settings. A clear report only means this prefix check found no matches.
