SKU Photo Matcher

Check your supplier catalogue against a folder of numbered product photos. See which SKUs have photos and which filenames need a closer look. Your CSV and filenames stay in your browser.

Choose the catalogue

Use a comma-separated UTF-8 file with a header and one row per SKU. The limit is 5,000 products and 2 MiB. Each SKU can contain up to 200 characters.

Choose the photo filenames

Choose files, choose an extracted photo folder, or paste one filename per line. A new selection replaces the list. Only filenames are read. Include up to 5,000 entries and 2 MiB of filename text.

For SKU 0012, use a name such as 0012-01.jpg. Start with the SKU, then a hyphen, underscore or space and a positive number of up to six digits. Supported extensions are JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP.

How the matcher decides

Filename Cleaner lowercases the SKU and joins its letters and numbers with your chosen separator. The matcher uses that same spelling. Leading zeros count, so 0012 and 12 can belong to different products.

If several SKUs produce the same prefix, check Filename Cleaner’s original-to-new mapping and the original photos. Rerun those SKUs in separate batches with their verified photos. If two images claim one position, review the shots and give each retained file a distinct number. Open the images in Image Contact Sheet to check the product and order.
