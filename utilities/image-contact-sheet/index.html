File preparation

Image Contact Sheet

Make a downloadable PNG sheet of product photos with numbered filename captions. Review a batch without opening each file individually.

Photos to review

Select up to 30 JPEG, PNG or WebP files, up to 32 MB each and 100 MB combined (1 MB = 1,000,000 bytes). Batches above 30 photos or 100 MB are rejected in full; split them before continuing. Use Move earlier and Move later below the preview to choose your listing order. The preview, numbered captions and downloaded PNG update together.

Contact sheet preview

Choose photos or try the example.

    Take this order to Filename Cleaner

    Download the ordered filenames and use Load filename list in Filename Cleaner, or copy and paste the list below. Then choose Product / SKU + numbers. Only photos shown on the sheet are included. Reordering a photo updates this list.

    Open Filename Cleaner

    The UTF-8 text file contains one original filename per line, without numbering or folder paths. It does not contain the photos. Download or copy again after changing the order.

    Review the batch before you send it

    Choose your strongest photo first, then arrange the supporting views with Move earlier and Move later. You can use the buttons with a mouse, touch or keyboard. Changing the number of columns keeps your chosen order; choosing new files starts a new batch.

    Download the sheet to share your chosen order. The sheet fits each image inside its cell without cropping. Long captions are shortened on the image; the list preserves the full names. Transparent areas appear white in the thumbnails. Your original photos and filenames stay unchanged.

    For submission checks, check the original image dimensions. Follow the full product-photo preparation guide for size checks, ordering and SKU names.

    Processing takes place on this device. Files you select stay in your browser.
