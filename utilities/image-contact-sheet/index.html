File preparation

Image Contact Sheet

Make a downloadable PNG sheet of product photos with numbered filename captions. Review a batch without opening each file individually.

Photos to review

Select up to 24 JPEG, PNG or WebP files, up to 32 MB each. Use Move earlier and Move later below the preview to choose your listing order. The preview, numbered captions and downloaded PNG update together.

Contact sheet preview

Choose photos or try the example.

    Review the batch before you send it

    Choose your strongest photo first, then arrange the supporting views with Move earlier and Move later. You can use the buttons with a mouse, touch or keyboard. Changing the number of columns keeps your chosen order; choosing new files starts a new batch.

    Download the sheet to share your chosen order. The sheet fits each image inside its cell without cropping. Long captions are shortened on the image; the list preserves the full names. Transparent areas appear white in the thumbnails. Your original photos and filenames stay unchanged.

    For submission checks, check the original image dimensions.

    Processing takes place on this device. Files you select stay in your browser.
