File preparation

Image Dimension Checker

Check a batch of product photos against minimum pixel dimensions and a maximum file size. See exactly which photos need attention before publishing.

Photos to review

Choose eBay to fill in its recommended-size check, or enter your own limits below.

This preset checks for at least 1600 pixels in both dimensions and no more than 12 MB per photo. It checks recommended size, not whether eBay will accept a listing: smaller photos may still be accepted. Rectangular photos are supported; no square crop is required. eBay photo guidance (checked 10 November 2026). Editing a limit switches to Custom limits.

Enter the limits for your destination. One MB is 1,000,000 bytes. Choose up to 30 JPEG, PNG or WebP photos, each up to 32 MB and 100 MB combined. Batches above 30 photos or 100 MB are rejected in full; split them before continuing.

Image checks

Choose photos or try the example.

Choose photos and valid limits to create a report.

The report lists photos needing attention first, with filenames, dimensions, exact file sizes and reasons, followed by passing photos. Unreadable files are marked as incomplete checks. It updates when you change valid limits and is unavailable while photos are loading or limits are invalid. Review filenames before sharing; the report contains no photo contents.

Make smaller photo copies

Use the minimum width, minimum height and maximum file size above. Optionally limit the longest edge below. Only photos exceeding a maximum are changed; photos already within all limits keep their original bytes in the ZIP.

Leave blank to reduce file size only. Modified copies are JPEGs: proportions and display orientation are preserved, with no crop or enlargement. Transparent areas become white. JPEG encoding can reduce detail and does not copy original EXIF metadata. Unchanged files retain their metadata. Originals stay untouched.

Up to 30 photos and 100 MB combined, each up to 32 MB and 40 megapixels. Resizing renders at most 16 megapixels at once. If the maximum conflicts with your minimum dimensions, or the file cannot fit without going below them, no ZIP is offered. The tool never treats a failed resize as a pass.

Choose photos and valid limits first.

Review the previews and measurements, then download the full checked batch and resize-report.txt. Copies receive numbered filenames to prevent collisions. Changing photos, limits or the preset clears the prepared download. Extract into a new folder and use these copies in the contact sheet and Filename Cleaner.

Which photo needs another look?

The example resets to Custom limits with an 800 × 800 pixel minimum and an 8 MB maximum. The front view is 1000 × 1000. The detail view is 1000 × 600, so its height falls short by 200 pixels. Select the eBay preset to recheck the same photos against the recommended-size target.

Dimensions describe the decoded image in its display orientation. Passing the size checks says nothing about focus, compression quality or a marketplace's other listing rules.
