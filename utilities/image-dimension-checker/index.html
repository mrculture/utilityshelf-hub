Image Dimension Checker

Check a batch of product photos against minimum pixel dimensions and a maximum file size. See exactly which photos need attention before publishing.

Photos to review

Choose eBay to fill in its recommended-size check, or enter your own limits below.

This preset checks for at least 1600 pixels in both dimensions and no more than 12 MB per photo. It checks recommended size, not whether eBay will accept a listing: smaller photos may still be accepted. Rectangular photos are supported; no square crop is required. eBay photo guidance (checked 10 November 2026). Editing a limit switches to Custom limits.

Enter the limits for your destination. One MB is 1,000,000 bytes. Choose up to 30 JPEG, PNG or WebP photos, each up to 32 MB.

Which photo needs another look?

The example resets to Custom limits with an 800 × 800 pixel minimum and an 8 MB maximum. The front view is 1000 × 1000. The detail view is 1000 × 600, so its height falls short by 200 pixels. Select the eBay preset to recheck the same photos against the recommended-size target.

Dimensions describe the decoded image in its display orientation. Passing the size checks says nothing about focus, compression quality or a marketplace's other listing rules.
