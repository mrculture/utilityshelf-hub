Privacy

UtilityShelf is designed around lightweight tools. Individual tools should explain whether processing happens locally in the browser.

Hosting providers may receive standard technical request data such as IP address, browser, requested page, and time of visit.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure page visits and loading performance across UtilityShelf. Our analytics integration does not submit the contents you enter into our tools.

Some guide pages may contain links to third-party websites or services. If a link is an affiliate or referral link, the third-party service may use cookies or similar technologies after you click the link to attribute the referral. UtilityShelf does not control those third-party sites or their privacy practices.

UtilityShelf may earn a commission from some third-party links at no extra cost to you. Affiliate relationships should not change the free browser-first utility experience.
