For online sellers

Prepare product photos

Check sizes, resize oversized photos when needed, then choose the listing order and download SKU-named copies with their filename mapping.

  1. 1 · Check & resize

    Check sizes and resize if needed

    Check your limits and save a photo report. If photos exceed your chosen maximums, prepare smaller copies, review the previews and download the checked-copy ZIP. Extract and recheck those copies.

    Check, resize and download a report
  2. 2 · Arrange

    Choose the photo order

    Choose the final checked files, including any resized copies. Move your strongest view first, download a contact sheet and copy the ordered filenames.

    Arrange a contact sheet
  3. 3 · Name & download

    Download SKU-named photos

    Paste the ordered list, review the SKU names and select those same checked files by their current filenames. Download renamed photo copies in a ZIP with the filename mapping included.

    Name photos and download ZIP

Photos stay in your browser. Resizing makes new JPEG copies and leaves your originals untouched. If you resize, use the extracted copies throughout the remaining steps. Keep the resize report, final photo report, contact sheet and renamed-photo ZIP together. Filename Cleaner matches by the selected files’ current names and preserves their bytes; it does not resize them again.

For supplier imports

Prepare a supplier CSV

Check the source file, match your destination columns, then review the exported CSV before importing.

  1. 1 · Check

    Check the file structure

    Find duplicate headers, quoting problems and rows with missing or extra fields. Correct the source before mapping.

    Check a CSV before importing
  2. 2 · Map

    Match your template

    Rename and reorder supplier columns to match your destination's headings, then download the mapped CSV.

    Map supplier columns
  3. 3 · Review

    Review formula warnings

    Check the downloaded CSV for values a spreadsheet might treat as formulas. Review any flags before using the file.

    Check CSV formulas

Files stay in your browser. Choose the file separately in each tool. After making changes, recheck the final export's structure and your destination's required fields.
