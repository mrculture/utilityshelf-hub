How to prepare cleaner links for social posts

Social posts often need short, readable, trustworthy links. When you copy a URL from an ad, search result, or email, it may include identifiers that add no value to your audience. Cleaning the link first makes the post tidier.

Clean before posting

Paste the URL into LinkClean Shelf, copy the cleaned result, and test the preview on the platform you plan to use.

Open LinkClean

Checklist

  1. Remove common tracking IDs from copied links.
  2. Keep page-specific parameters if they select the right item, language, or filter.
  3. Check the platform preview image, title, and description.
  4. Avoid shortened links if the audience needs to inspect the destination.
  5. For your own campaign, intentionally add your own measurement tags after cleanup.

Cleaner does not mean shorter every time

A clean link can still be long if the destination genuinely needs parameters. The goal is to remove unnecessary tracking clutter, not flatten every URL.

FAQ

Will a cleaned link always show the same preview? Usually, but social platforms cache previews. Test important posts before publishing.

Should I clean affiliate links? Do not remove parameters that are required for attribution or disclosure compliance.
